Questions from schools

Good to know.

What devices do students need?

Gallop runs in any modern web browser — Chromebooks, iPads, laptops, or desktops. There's nothing to install, and it works on the devices most schools already have.

How do students log in and get rostered?

We help you add students and classes during onboarding. Students sign in simply and place into their real level in each subject the first time they use it — no lengthy setup for teachers.

How do you handle student-data privacy?

Gallop is COPPA-compliant, shows no ads, and never sells student data. We're glad to review and complete your school or district's data-privacy agreement as part of getting started.

Is there a pilot?

Yes. Most schools start with a small pilot — a class or a grade — before rolling out more widely, so you can see the fit with your students first.

Does this replace our teachers or curriculum?

No — it's built to supplement classroom instruction, not replace it. Gallop reinforces what you teach with a guided lesson before every practice set, and adds the real-world skills in The Lab that most curricula don't reach.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per student and scales with your size, with classroom, grade, and whole-school options. Tell us your enrollment through the form below and we'll send a quote — usually the same week.
